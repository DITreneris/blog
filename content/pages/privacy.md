## What we collect

The Site is a **static website**. We do not offer user accounts, comments, or newsletter signup on the blog itself.

We may receive information you choose to send us (for example, by email). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

## Cookies and tracking {#cookies}

On **production** builds of the Site (hosted on Vercel), we use **Vercel Web Analytics** to understand aggregate traffic (such as page views and referrers). Analytics runs only when enabled at deploy time; local development builds do not load the analytics script by default.

Vercel may process technical data such as IP address, browser type, and page URL as part of this service. See [Vercel Web Analytics documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics) for details.

You can limit tracking by using browser privacy settings, disabling JavaScript, or using an ad or analytics blocker.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies on the blog.

## Third-party links

Articles and the footer may link to external sites (for example [promptanatomy.app](https://www.promptanatomy.app/), social profiles, or partner properties). Those sites have their own privacy practices; review their policies before sharing personal information.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Site after changes means you accept the revised policy.
