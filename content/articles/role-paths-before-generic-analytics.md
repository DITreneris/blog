*Part 2 — after soft launch.* Part 1 covers click-and-do pedagogy: [Click-and-Do Before Slide Decks](/articles/click-and-do-data-analysis-soft-launch/). Agent depth after analysis practice is a tester grant in [3A Before You Build an Agent](/articles/3a-before-you-build-an-agent/).

One "data analysis" course fails six jobs. Sales needs forecasts and pipeline honesty. People teams need retention language without inventing HR metrics. Executives need risk-aware summaries, not another chart dump. When every learner pastes the same generic analyst prompt, the failure mode is polished wrongness that looks like progress.

On **2026-07-25**, [promptanatomy.app](https://www.promptanatomy.app/) shipped **v1.4.7**—the Data Analysis path. For hub checkout and Modules 1–6, see [Shipping Prompt Anatomy](/articles/shipping-prompt-anatomy/). For how blog vs training fit, start with [The Prompt Anatomy Ecosystem Map](/articles/prompt-anatomy-ecosystem-map/).

## Six roles, one analysis spine

Module 7 teaches the same analysis habits: name sources, structure the ask, verify before external send. The entry choice changed. Learners pick an organizational role; copyable prompts and practice framing adapt to that path—not one default voice.

| Role path | Primary OUTPUT shape |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **Sales** | KPI read, trend, and forecast language |
| **Marketing** | Channel, sentiment, and campaign readouts |
| **IT / engineering** | Schema, cleanup, and pipeline steps |
| **People / HR** | Funnel, retention, and pulse language |
| **Management** | Executive summary with explicit risk |
| **Other / internal data** | Universal templates with named placeholders |

The spine stays shared so remediation and Module 8 checks still make sense. The prompts stop pretending every learner is a business analyst. Six-block fluency from [The Six-Block Prompt System](/articles/six-block-prompt-system/) remains the foundation—role paths specialize Output and Quality, they do not replace Meta through Advanced.

## Role-fit beats generic packs

Generic analyst copy fails when the job is sales forecast language or HR retention wording—even if the analysis spine is sound. Soft launch made that clear: diagrams beat walls of prose, and v1.4.7 keeps the click-and-do pattern from Part 1 with shorter copy-run-check loops, clearer gates, and interactive schemas as **instruments**—step through them—not slide titles. Role-adapted copyables answer "this does not sound like my job."

That is practice inside the training product. [The Model Is Not the System](/articles/the-model-is-not-the-system/) applies: the lesson host assembles; ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini execute; your runtime and owners stay on your side.

## The Northline mistake, role edition

Northline's enablement lead (composite) rolled one "analyst" prompt pack to sales, marketing, and HR. Pass rates on Module 3 looked fine. A regional sales deck still shipped a fabricated conversion figure because nobody practiced the sales-path habit: sources named, forecast labeled as estimate, human verify before external send. The fix: make **role-shaped analyze-before-create** mandatory before teams treat training completion as ship-ready analytics. That failure mode maps to [10 Signs Your Company Is Vibe Prompting](/articles/10-signs-your-company-is-vibe-prompting/).

## Guardrails

- A Tier 3 certificate or finished Module 9 capstone does not replace workflow IDs, RACI, or eval gates—use [Grounding AI Outputs](/articles/grounding-ai-outputs/) and [Evaluation Hooks for AI Workflows](/articles/evaluation-hooks-for-ai-workflows/) on real workflows.
- Do not paste training copy into procurement decks; cite owned metrics per [AI Procurement Freeze](/articles/ai-procurement-freeze/).
- Pick the role path that matches the job; do not collect all six as proof of maturity.
- Plans and access stay on [promptanatomy.app/#pricing](https://www.promptanatomy.app/#pricing)—Core assumes Modules 1–6 before the Data Analysis specialization.
