## The brief is not the image tool

Content AI System, at promptanatomy.space, is a browser brief for a marketing image. You fill the fields and copy a prompt. The picture is made in an image tool you already use. The course stays on promptanatomy.app.

The builder assembles the instruction. It does not accept the picture. That boundary is the same one in [The Model Is Not the System](/articles/the-model-is-not-the-system/). Create is the marketing spoke on [The Prompt Anatomy Ecosystem Map](/articles/prompt-anatomy-ecosystem-map/). This note is the read. The page is where you build the prompt.

## One brief, filled

The English page publishes this sample. The form behind it asks for Campaign goal, Audience, Platform, Tone, Subject / object, Style, Lighting, Camera, Aspect ratio, Color palette, and an optional Headline and Call to action.

```text
Professional marketing visual, cognac leather handbag on travertine. Warm directional sunlight, ultra-detailed, highest quality, professional composition, Aspect ratio: 1:1. Created for marketing purpose: awareness, targeting B2B marketers on LinkedIn. Mood: premium.
```

The published sample uses these words:

- “cognac leather handbag on travertine” for the subject
- “awareness” for the campaign goal
- “targeting B2B marketers on LinkedIn” for audience and platform
- “Mood: premium” for tone
- “Aspect ratio: 1:1”
- “Warm directional sunlight” is the sample’s light. The form’s lighting choices are Cinematic lighting, Soft daylight, Golden Hour, Studio lighting, and Neon lighting, so that phrase is not a dropdown label copied into the line.

Style, Camera, Color palette, Headline, and Call to action are on the form and unused in this sample. Headline and Call to action are optional. The quality line “ultra-detailed, highest quality, professional composition” is in the published sample and is not one of those field names.

## What a person still checks

Copy the prompt, paste it into the image tool, and review the picture before it ships. The image tool can still miss the brief. A one-line request such as “luxury handbag, professional photo” can return a black bag on marble, a logo the brief never named, or a 16:9 crop. This sample already named cognac leather on travertine and a 1:1 frame. That miss is what the tool can still do. It is not a recorded customer incident.

- The subject is the cognac leather handbag on travertine, not a substituted bag or stone.
- No logo or claim appears that the brief did not name.
- The frame is 1:1, the ratio the sample asked for.
- If a headline was written, the words on the image are that headline.

Color palette is on the form and unused in this sample, so this check does not grade a palette. The picture still comes from the image tool, then a person checks it.
