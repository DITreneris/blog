*Part 3 — gated agent practice.* Part 1 is click-and-do pedagogy: [Click-and-Do Before Slide Decks](/articles/click-and-do-data-analysis-soft-launch/). Part 2 is role-fit on the analysis path: [Role Paths Before Generic Analytics](/articles/role-paths-before-generic-analytics/).

The figure's **80/15/5** split is a planning heuristic, not a measured mix and not an ROI claim. Assign **each process** a band before anyone builds. Control maturity is a different axis, in [From Prompt to Agent](/articles/from-prompt-to-agent/).

## Three processes, three bands

| Band | Trigger | Why this band |
|------|---------|----------------|
| **Automate** | A form arrives with an email, and the domain is not free mail. | Rules fire. No one judges the row. The miss is calling that flow an agent. |
| **Assist** | A customer asks for a concession. | No cap is written down. A named person checks the amount, then sends. |
| **Autonomize** | A refund request, the order already delivered, and the amount at or under a cap the team wrote down. | That cap is a threshold you set, not a Prompt Anatomy price. Under the cap, write an audit row and refund. Over the cap, escalate. |

Northline's enablement lead (composite) shipped the concession email as Autonomize. The credit in the mail was fabricated. The name on the project was not the failure. Skipping the confirm was. A written cap plus an audit row is what moves a refund to Autonomize. The concession had neither.

A research job that also sends is the same miss. Split find from send in [A Shortlist, Not Another Inbox](/articles/a-shortlist-not-another-inbox/). A ticket-type split between the concession email and the refund is a router, not a third agent. Bounded tasks and escalation live in [How to Design an AI Agent Workflow](/articles/how-to-design-an-ai-agent-workflow/).

Write three of your processes and assign a band. If all three land on Autonomize, the list is hype.

Finishing the Agent path shows judgment inside the drills. The system you run in production still belongs to you. For how this site and the training host fit, start with [The Prompt Anatomy Ecosystem Map](/articles/prompt-anatomy-ecosystem-map/). The drills are not the runtime: [The Model Is Not the System](/articles/the-model-is-not-the-system/).
