Sales outreach plus AI tempts teams to automate sends. **Guardrails first** protect brand, deliverability, and compliance.

## Guardrails

| Control | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| Human send only | Accountability |
| Daily send cap per rep | Deliverability |
| Approved snippet library | Consistent claims |
| Opt-out / suppression list check | Compliance |
| No fabricated references | Trust |

## Workflow

CRM segment → model drafts personalized opening from approved facts → rep edits → send → log outcome to CRM.

Measure reply quality and overrides, not emails generated.
