Team Rituals for AI Implementation

1 min read · Implementation Notes · 2026

Key takeaway

Rituals beat one-off trainings—short, recurring forums maintain prompts, context, and ownership.

Tools do not maintain themselves. Rituals keep workflows honest after launch.

Suggested rituals

Ritual Frequency Purpose
Workflow office hours Weekly in pilot Unblock owners, capture friction
Eval review Biweekly Regressions before they reach customers
Change log standup When releasing prompt/context Who needs to know
Risk forum Monthly Tie to risk cadence

Rules

  • Time-box to 30–45 minutes.
  • Bring one real failure per session.
  • Decisions logged with owner and date.
