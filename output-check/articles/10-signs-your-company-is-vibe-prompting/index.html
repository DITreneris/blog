10 Signs Your Company Is Vibe Prompting

2 min read · Implementation Notes · 2026

Key takeaway

If outcomes depend on who asked and which tool they opened, document one workflow and measure it for thirty days.

Vibe prompting means outcomes depend on who asks, which tool they open, and informal know-how—not on documented workflows.

Signs to watch for

  1. No shared prompt library or version control. Good prompts live in private chats; changes are not traceable.

  2. Success stories do not reproduce across teams. One region’s wins do not transfer with the same inputs elsewhere.

  3. Compliance learns about AI from incidents, not design reviews. Handbooks are silent while shadow use grows.

  4. Every department bought a different copilot. Integration debt and duplicate data paths have no owner.

  5. “Just ask ChatGPT” is the strategy. No defined outputs, owners, or quality bar for customer-facing work.

  6. No evaluation set for high-risk outputs. Model or prompt changes ship without regression tests.

  7. Context lives in people's heads, not systems. Policy and nuance sit in threads, not retrievable stores.

  8. Agents run without audit trails. You cannot reconstruct who approved customer-facing output.

  9. Executives see demos, operators see chaos. Pilots look fine; frontline staff rework output nightly.

  10. More tools arrive before more structure. Tool count rises; outcome variance rises with it.

30-day remediation

Week Action
1 Pick one workflow; write outcome and acceptable error rate
2 Document context sources and human review gates
3 Run 20 real cases; classify failures (policy, fact, format)
4 Report pass rate and scope decision to leadership
